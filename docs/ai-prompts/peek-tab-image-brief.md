# Edge Tab Image Generation Brief

## App Context

**App Name:** Talking Music League (TML)
**Purpose:** A companion app for Music League players. Lets family/friend groups discuss rounds, track submissions and votes, play minigames, and celebrate awards.

**Vibe:** Music-obsessed, nostalgic, warm, playful. Think record stores, mixtapes, vinyl collections, and friendly competition.

**League Name Example:** "Revenge of the Hip Jammers"
**Round Name Example:** "Primetime"
**Display Format:** `League Name — Round Name`

---

## What These Images Are For

A **pull-tab** that sits on the right edge of the mobile screen. Users tap or drag it to slide out a sidebar panel showing round details, progress, and minigames.

The tab should look like something you'd physically pull or grab — like pulling a record from a bin or sliding a cassette out of a shelf.

**Placement:** Fixed to right edge of screen, vertically centered
**Interaction:** Tap or drag left to open panel

---

## Design Concepts

### Option A: Cassette Spine Label
A cassette tape spine (the thin edge you see when cassettes are stacked). Features handwritten or typewriter-style text with the league/round name.

- **Orientation:** Vertical (rotated 90° so text reads bottom-to-top, like a real cassette spine)
- **Style:** Handwritten marker, typewriter, or label-maker aesthetic
- **Details:** Slightly worn edges, maybe a small torn corner or tape residue

### Option B: Record Bin Divider
A record store divider card (the alphabetical tabs in vinyl bins). Could show a genre, letter, or the round theme.

- **Orientation:** Vertical tab sticking up
- **Style:** Cardboard texture, hand-lettered or stamped text
- **Details:** Slightly bent corners, coffee stain, thumbed edges

---

## Size Requirements

### Cassette Spine (Option A)
- **Dimensions:** ~40px wide × 280-320px tall (narrow and long)
- **Aspect ratio:** Approximately 1:7 or 1:8
- The narrow width is intentional — cassette spines are thin

### Record Divider (Option B)
- **Dimensions:** ~50px wide × 200-240px tall
- **Aspect ratio:** Approximately 1:4 or 1:5
- Slightly wider than cassette, more like a tab

## Suggested Image Colors

### For Light Mode Tab
- **Background:** Cream/off-white (`#f5f0e6`) or kraft paper (`#d4c4a8`)
- **Text:** Navy (`#0a1a2f`) or dark brown (`#3d2b1f`)
- **Accent:** Coral (`#ff6f61`) for a highlight stripe or dot
- **Wear/aging:** Light brown stains, slightly yellowed

### For Dark Mode Tab
- **Background:** Charcoal (`#1a1a1a`) or deep navy (`#0f1c2d`)
- **Text:** Cream (`#f5f0e6`) or light blue (`#bfd6f2`)
- **Accent:** Coral (`#ff6f61`) or mint (`#18e0a8`)
- **Style:** Could be more "new" looking, or aged with subtle wear

## Text Content for Image

**Primary text (choose one format):**
- `Revenge of the Hip Jammers — Primetime`
- `REVENGE OF THE HIP JAMMERS`
  `— Primetime —`

**Or abbreviated:**

## Dark Mode vs Light Mode

Please generate **both versions** if possible:
1. **Light mode:** Warm, aged paper/cardboard aesthetic
2. **Dark mode:** Same concept but inverted — dark background, light text

When submitting your prompt, specify which mode you want:
- "Generate the LIGHT MODE version"
- "Generate the DARK MODE version"
