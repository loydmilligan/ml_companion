## Season Summary

Season 1 featured themes from 9 different members, covering everything from guilty pleasures to global music, nostalgia trips to anti-favorites. The creative theme titles (like "Finding Emos" and "I like big butts and a can of limes") set the tone for what would become a tradition of clever, punny round names.
