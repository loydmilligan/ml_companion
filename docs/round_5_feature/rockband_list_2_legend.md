## **General Notes**

### **Tiers and Metadata**

* **Latest Game Bias:** Tiers and genres are based on the latest available game entry. (Exception: The three *RB1* exclusive songs use tiers from the Rock Band Database/RBDB).  
* **Artist Names:** Names appear exactly as they do in-game, even if technically incorrect (e.g., "Mute Math" instead of "Mutemath").  
* **Origin Discrepancies:** For songs appearing in track packs (CTP1, MTP, Unplugged, or Blitz), the "Origin" may be listed as general era DLC (e.g., *RB1/2* or *RB3* DLC) because they share the same technical era.  
* **Difficulty Scaling:** Song tiers are based on internal difficulty values. These systems changed significantly between *RB1*, *RB2*, and *RB3*. RBN songs use a tier system rather than a specific value, which is why unique RBN tiers were created for this sheet.

### **Availability and Delistings**

* **RBN Limitations:** RBN1 songs are not playable in *RB1*.  
* **Delisted Content:** \* All RBN1 and RBN2 songs are **DELISTED** for *RB2* and *RB3* (only those "rewound" for *RB4* remain available).  
  * All *The Beatles: Rock Band* (TBRB) DLC is **DELISTED**.  
  * All *Rock Band Unplugged* DLC is **DELISTED**.  
  * All mobile versions (*Mobile*, *iOS*, *Reloaded*) and their DLC are **DELISTED**.  
* **RBN2 Platform Note:** RBN2 songs were never released on Wii. A "Yes" in the *RB3* column implies playability on PS3 and Xbox 360 only.  
* **Rock Band VR (RBVR):** Exclusive songs for *RBVR* had an extremely limited release on *RB4*. Due to a lack of verifiable information, these have been omitted. Origin metadata for *RBVR* songs often points to *RB4* DLC as it is the more prominent platform.

### **Release Dates**

* **RBN Dates:** Songs on the Rock Band Network were often available before their listed date; the date shown reflects when they were officially added to the primary Rock Band Store.
